# Simulation media credits

These files are used only by FirstLink's simulation mode to stand in for witness uploads. The photos are real San Diego-area
wildfire photographs from earlier fires. They do not depict the staged La Jolla incident, and the app labels them as simulation media.

The photos were resized to at most 1280 px and re-encoded as JPEG (no other edits).

`voice-parking-deck.mp3` and `voice-canyon-rim.mp3` are synthetic voices generated with ElevenLabs text-to-speech
(premade voices "Chris" and "Sarah", model `eleven_flash_v2_5`) from scripted witness lines. They are not recordings of real people.
