# Simulation media credits

These files are used only by FirstLink's simulation mode to stand in for witness uploads. The photos are real San Diego-area
wildfire photographs from earlier fires. They do not depict the staged La Jolla incident, and the app labels them as simulation media.

| File | Source | Author | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lilac-fire-road.jpg` | [Lilac Fire crossing a road](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lilac_Fire_crossing_a_road.jpg), 2017-12-09 | Cal Fire San Diego | Public domain |
| `witch-creek-ridge.jpg` | [Witch Creek Fire on Bernardo Mt, Escondido, California](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Witch_Creek_Fire_on_Bernardo_Mt,_Escondido,_California.jpg), 2007-10-23 | Chaparralian | CC0 |
| `sd-skyline-smoke.jpg` | [San Diego skyline against smoke from wildfires Oct 2007](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:San_Diego_skyline_against_smoke_from_wildfires_Oct_2007.jpg), 2007-10-23 | Kat Miner | [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/) |

The photos were resized to at most 1280 px and re-encoded as JPEG (no other edits).

`voice-parking-deck.mp3` and `voice-canyon-rim.mp3` are synthetic voices generated with ElevenLabs text-to-speech
(premade voices "Chris" and "Sarah", model `eleven_flash_v2_5`) from scripted witness lines. They are not recordings of real people.
