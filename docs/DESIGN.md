# FirstLink design direction

The user supplied a Customer.io style reference, described as dark spruce forest meeting cream paper. Apply this to an original product, not a copy of Customer.io's logo or content.

- Product name: **FirstLink**. Promise: "The first minutes, connected." Core explanation: turn witness reports into one shared incident brief and coordinate nearby support.
- Spruce surfaces: #00191c deepest, #032125 main dark/text, #0b363b raised dark panels; muted #437278 and #a1c2c6. Cream #fffcf6 is the default light canvas. White panels have 1px #ebebeb borders.
- Bright #abffae is reserved for primary pill actions and focus glows. Soft blue #e2f4ff and pale green #eafde8 may support contextual panels; avoid treating those colors as proof of safety.
- Use Inter variable as the permitted Saans substitute, around weight 475. Headings never exceed 600. Display 64–96px desktop / 40–52px mobile, line-height 1; body 16px / 1.38–1.6. Editorial spacing and calm authority.
- 2px corners on all non-button elements, full pill buttons, no drop shadows, hairline borders. Focus uses a 4px colored glow. Card padding 24–32px; section gaps 80–96px. Centered max-width 1200px.
- Sticky 64px header, original compact geometric mark and lowercase firstlink wordmark. Visible "Simulation" / "Staged incident" labels. Avoid generic SaaS gradients, giant rounded cards, emoji icons, busy analytics, or fake operational claims.
- Landing: dark hero with oversized left-aligned headline and a beautifully composed live product preview to the right; cream explanation/action section. Actual incident workspace is the star, not a lengthy marketing page.
- Dashboard: dark header/side framing, cream workspace, clear incident heading, compact metrics derived from real demo data, incident scene/map panel, evidence-linked brief, witness feed, and nearby volunteer cards. Carefully designed empty/loading/error states.
- Map is a schematic of the staged scene with streets, building footprint, sources and staging marker; explicitly label it as a demo map. No invented safe navigation or emergency boundaries. No decorative illustrations. Photos belong to witness evidence.
- All buttons and navigation must work. Responsive tablet/mobile, readable contrast, keyboard navigation, visible labels and focus states. Do not expose backend configuration details in normal product flows.
